How Plastic Injection Molding Works

Think of it like an ultra-precise, heavy-duty waffle iron for durable plastic parts:

1

We Build the Mold (The Tool)

We carve your exact design out of heavy-duty hardened steel[cite: 3]. Once built, this mold is 100% your legal asset forever—we never charge recurring license fees[cite: 3].

2

The Press Injects & Forms

Raw plastic resin pellets are melted down, injected into the mold under hundreds of tons of pressure, cooled in seconds, and ejected with perfect consistency[cite: 3, 4].

3

Direct Delivery to Your Door

We box, label, inspect every piece, and truck them straight from Guadalajara into the United States without ocean port delays or surprise duties[cite: 3].
